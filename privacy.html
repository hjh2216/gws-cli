Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

gws-cli is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner to access their own Google account data from the command line on their own computer.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app may access Google Drive files, Gmail messages and Google Calendar events of the signed-in account, through the Google APIs.

How data is used

Data is used only to run commands the owner issues on their own computer. It is not sent to any server operated by this app.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer only. No Google user data is stored elsewhere.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
